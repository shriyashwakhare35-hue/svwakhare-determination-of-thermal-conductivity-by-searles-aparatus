Equations & Principles

1. Thermal Conductivity Formula:

$$ K = \frac{m \cdot s \cdot (\theta_4 - \theta_3) \cdot d}{A \cdot (\theta_1 - \theta_2) \cdot t} $$

2. Cross-sectional Area:

$$ A = \frac{\pi \cdot D^2}{4} $$

3. Vernier Reading:

$$ TR = MSR + (VSR \times LC) $$
